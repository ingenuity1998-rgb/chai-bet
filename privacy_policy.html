Core Privacy Pledge: Chai Bet does not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. We do not track your browsing history, read private messages, or log keystrokes.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

Chai Bet is designed with minimal data collection principles. All core functionality operates directly on your device:

  • User-Configured Preferences: We store your buddy's name and UPI ID, chosen focus hours, and your selected snack bet tier locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences.
  • App Usage Data: The app monitors which applications are in the foreground strictly to determine if a blacklisted app (e.g., Instagram, YouTube) has been opened during your self-selected focus window. This data never leaves your device.

2. Prominent Disclosure: Android AccessibilityService API

In accordance with Google Play's User Data and Accessibility policies, Chai Bet makes the following explicit disclosure:

  • Purpose of Accessibility API: Chai Bet requires the AccessibilityService API strictly to detect when user-selected trigger applications enter the foreground during your active focus hours.
  • Scope of Use: The Accessibility Service is used solely to detect the package name of the active foreground application to trigger the user's voluntary focus overlay.
  • What We NEVER Do: The Accessibility Service does not read, intercept, store, or transmit any on-screen text, form inputs, personal messages, passwords, financial account details, or keystrokes.

3. Financial and Payment Information

Chai Bet does not directly process, store, or handle sensitive financial credentials such as credit card numbers, debit card PINs, or bank account passwords:

  • All voluntary chai bet settlements are executed by deep-linking to external, RBI-regulated UPI payment applications installed on your device (such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM).
  • Chai Bet merely formats standard upi://pay intents with the recipient VPA and amount configured by you.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

All focus session history and preferences reside strictly within your device's local application sandbox. You can permanently delete all stored data at any time by clearing application data in your device Settings or by uninstalling the application.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected with a revised Effective Date at the top of this page.
